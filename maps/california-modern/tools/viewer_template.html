About this map

15.5 km between neighboring hex centers. Source geography is generalized to the game grid; small islands and key lakes use a minimum of one tile. Named wine, oil and mineral districts anchor resource placement. Gold is omitted. The 12 suggested starts use freshwater, fertility, expansion space and spacing; they are inspection markers, not fixed civilization spawns.

Database and geography checks are included in validation_report.json. An in-game load test is still required. See the landform audit, the forest audit, the resource audit, and installation and limitations.

Terrain: USGS / NOAA / INEGI via Mapzen terrain tiles. Coastlines, lakes and borders: Natural Earth. Rivers: Natural Earth + USGS NHD (DWR and supplemental services). Ecoregions: US EPA. Sources and method. Entire inspector works offline.
