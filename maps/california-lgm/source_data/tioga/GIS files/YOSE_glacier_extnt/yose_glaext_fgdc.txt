        Title: Glacial Extent during Tioga Glaciation (Yosemite Area)
        Geospatial_Data_Presentation_Form: Vector Digital Data Set (Polygon)
    Description:
      Abstract: Map of glacial extent in Yosemite National Park and vicinity as it looked circa 20,000 years ago during the Last Glacial Maximum (Tioga) Glaciation.
      Purpose: This map was produced, over a period of more than 50 years, by scientists from the US Geological Survey and the National Park Service, based on extensive field mapping and remote sensing analyses.  This is the extent shown in the USGS Scientific Investigation Series Map "Extent of the Last Glacial Maximum (Tioga) Glaciation in Yosemite National Park and Vicinity, California" by Wahrhaftig et al, 2018.
