About these maps

84 × 54 standard grid; north up; 9 km between hex centers. All eight main islands. This is an ocean-heavy regional map: six suggested maritime starts, with asymmetric island sizes. All islands are connected by coast-water routes for early ships; deep channel tiles on these routes are deliberate gameplay changes.

LGM scenario: ~21 ka, sea level −125 m, cooling 4.5°C, lower modeled lowland rainfall and downslope vegetation zones. Maui–Lānaʻi–Molokaʻi form Maui Nui; Kahoʻolawe remains separate.

One impassable Snow Mountain hex represents the Mauna Kea ice cap. No continental ice sheet, sea ice, or invented pluvial lakes. Mauna Loa glaciation remains uncertain and is not asserted.

Rainfall-based vegetation is generalized potential cover with a few explicit pasture, plantation and quarry adjustments. Both versions use modern crops and introduced livestock/wildlife as gameplay resources. Start markers are suggestions; they are not forced native scenario starts.
